# 04 - Lead / Staff Engineer Coverage: 8 to 12 Years

## System Design Expectations

- turn ambiguous goals into workload, SLO, security, data, and cost requirements
- estimate QPS, payload, storage, bandwidth, and growth
- choose boundaries and data ownership
- design APIs/events and compatibility strategy
- plan failure isolation, capacity, observability, and recovery
- show migration stages rather than only an end-state diagram

## Reliability

- SLI/SLO/error budgets
- multi-zone failure and graceful degradation
- capacity models and admission control
- backup/restore objectives: RPO and RTO
- incident command, mitigation, communication, and learning review
- dependency and deployment blast radius

## Platform Tradeoffs

- build vs buy using lifetime cost and lock-in
- synchronous vs asynchronous flows
- relational vs document vs key-value vs search/vector stores
- containers, orchestration, autoscaling, and startup/readiness behavior
- shared platform vs team autonomy

## Staff-Level Influence

- author decision records and paved-road standards
- lead cross-team migrations without stopping delivery
- create measurable quality/reliability improvements
- recognize organizational coupling hidden inside architecture
- coach decision-making rather than becoming an approval bottleneck

## Interview Evidence

Prepare examples with scope, constraints, alternatives, decision, measurable outcome, failure/learning, and what you would change now.
